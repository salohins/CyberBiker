One source per game clip. Source format: 44.1kHz, 24-bit stereo AIFF.
Game files: 48kHz, 24-bit stereo PCM WAV. Only gain, tail trimming, a 1ms
start fade and a 45ms point / 60ms reward tail fade are applied. There are
no added impact or click layers, echo, distortion, EQ or generated tones.

The original AIFFs are retained locally in Logs/score_audio_clean/wobbleboxx.
Audio imports use preloaded, decompressed PCM for short playback latency.
