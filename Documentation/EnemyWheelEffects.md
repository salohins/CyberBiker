Enemy wheel effects use the existing Cartoon FX smoke atlas and rolling cloud treatment, with larger connected clouds. Both enemy prefabs now use size 1.15 (previously 0.5), lifetime 0.65 seconds, emission 130 and opacity 0.9. Smoke and skid sparks begin at 20 degrees of actual lean and reach full intensity at 30 degrees; release hysteresis is 3 degrees. Straight riding does not emit skid particles.

Sparks use a dedicated EnemyTireSparks material at glow intensity 1.2. Width is 0.105, streak length 4, lifetime 0.22 seconds and emission 70, with a hard-lean boost of 2. The shared shader's Inspector glow range now extends to 12. The player's material remains at 4.5 and its effect settings are unchanged. Existing particle caps, distance culling, pause and cleanup remain active.

Tune each enemy prefab's Tire Contact Particles component for smoke size/lifetime/emission and lean thresholds, and Assets/Art/Materials/EnemyTireSparks.mat for spark glow. EnemyRidingSetup preserves these defaults when configuring prefabs again.

Native Unity captures at 5, 15 and 30 degrees verify no smoke/sparks at 5 or 15 degrees, and visible smoke/sparks on sharp 30-degree leans. A rendered comparison with sparks hidden confirms visible spark contribution from the gameplay camera at a 35-unit forward gap in the adjacent lane. Straight riding and return to straight stop emission; disabling clears particles. Captures and the report are in EnemyWheelEffectsValidation. Navigation, enemy movement, the player prefab and the shared particle runtime were verified unchanged for this visual update.

Spark glow was reduced from 8.5 to 1.2 after play feedback, with fewer overlapping streaks and a shorter spark lifetime to avoid the combined white flash. The enemy material also uses White Core 0.1, giving warmer sparks with much less white bloom. The shader defaults to the previous White Core 0.75 for the player. The smoke settings remain the same.

Activation was raised to 20 degrees after play feedback so small weaving does not leave continuous smoke clouds. Emission stops below 17 degrees as the turn settles; existing particles then finish their short lifetime. Cloud size and the softened spark appearance are preserved.

Smoke now tapers emission over 0.16 seconds after the lean releases. Existing clouds keep simulating in world space, progressively lose 55% of their inherited speed over their lifetime, roll outward with increasing noise, expand slightly and fade from early in life. They fall behind the moving bike rather than following it at almost full speed and then disappearing. This uses Unity's native [Velocity over Lifetime Speed Modifier](https://docs.unity3d.com/es/current/Manual/PartSysVelOverLifeModule.html), with zero new per-particle C# updates. Enemy prefabs enable the new airflow settings; their defaults are zero so the existing player smoke profile stays unchanged.

The release preview tracks the same live cloud after the turn: it survives the emission stop, falls another 4.57 metres behind the bike and reduces its alpha from 200 to 101 during the early release. Time-separated captures show the trail moving away and thinning before settling completely. Hard-turn activation and softened spark settings remain in effect.
